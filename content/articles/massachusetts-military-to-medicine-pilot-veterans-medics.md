The Healey-Driscoll administration announced a proposed Military to Medicine pilot on November 10 at a Public Health Council meeting, aiming to help veterans move into health care jobs while easing hospital staffing shortages.

The Department of Public Health and the Executive Office of Veterans Services presented the plan, under which up to 10 eligible veterans who previously worked as military medics could perform certain medication tasks at participating DPH-licensed hospitals. The state would advance new regulations to create the pathway, and the program builds on a Veterans Health Administration model.

Eligibility rules, timelines and participating hospitals were not detailed in coverage reviewed for this article.
