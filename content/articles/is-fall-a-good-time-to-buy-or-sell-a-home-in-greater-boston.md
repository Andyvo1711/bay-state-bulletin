Fall may favor buyers in Greater Boston, Boston.com reported September 17. Using 2025 Massachusetts Association of Realtors data, median single-family prices peaked in June at $1,007,000 (reflecting contracts signed in April) and bottomed in December at $875,000, reflecting contracts negotiated in October. September's median was $880,000 and October's $905,000.

Realtor Melvin A. Vieira Jr. of RE/MAX Real Estate Center said the market now has two buying seasons, "the spring and the fall." Chuck Silverston of Gibson Sotheby's International Realty said the market is "much less forgiving of aspirational pricing," and noted that some neighborhoods see heavy listings right after Labor Day. Zillow data show 2025 homes sold in late May fetched about 3.4% more, roughly $25,300.
