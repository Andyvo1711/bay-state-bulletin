Soufflé Therapeutics announced an oversubscribed $135 million Series B on October 13, led by Juvia Group. New backers included TCG Crossover, BB Biotech, Fidelity and Balyasny, along with a leading sovereign wealth fund.

The proceeds will move three lead programs into clinical studies, targeting facioscapulohumeral muscular dystrophy, heart failure and metabolic disease, and fund more preclinical work. The company's REMEDY platform identifies receptors unique to a target cell type and builds binders, such as monoclonal antibodies, that carry an siRNA payload. Soufflé raised a $200 million Series A in October 2025, led by Bessemer Venture Partners. Most reports describe the company as Boston-based; one placed it in Watertown.
