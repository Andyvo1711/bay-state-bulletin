Massachusetts' unemployment rate fell to 4.3% in August, its lowest level since March 2025, the state said September 18, as the national rate stood at 4.1%. "The unemployment rate fell to 4.3% in August," said Mark Rembert, chief economist at the state Department of Economic Research.

Payrolls, however, fell: preliminary estimates showed a loss of 7,100 jobs in August, though the state was up 8,300 jobs over the year. Labor force participation held at 65.3%. Local unemployment rates decreased in all twelve labor market areas compared with July. The September release later revised the August job loss to 5,600.
