The Massachusetts Department of Revenue reported preliminary August collections of $3.148 billion, $360 million or 12.9% more than August 2025, Commissioner Geoffrey Snyder announced.

Income tax, the largest component, totaled $1.900 billion, up $186 million or 10.8% from a year earlier, and withholding came in at $1.710 billion, up $62 million or 3.7%.
