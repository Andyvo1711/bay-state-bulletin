The University of Massachusetts Amherst held a ceremony in mid-September to mark the official opening of its $125 million Computer Science Laboratories building, home to the Manning College of Information and Computer Sciences.

Lt. Gov. Kim Driscoll attended, and Chancellor Javier Reyes spoke about the facility's role in advancing the university's academic mission. Although the building had already been in use since the fall 2025 semester, the event served as the formal celebration. Construction began with a groundbreaking in September 2022.

The building is the first structure on the Amherst campus to earn LEED Platinum certification from the U.S. Green Building Council.
