A simple request should be one of the easiest visits to get right, which is what makes this report on Colors Nail Bar worth a closer look. The client asked for something clean and classic, short square white nails, and left disappointed with a result that did not match the request, at a moment when it mattered most. The good news is that everything here comes down to careful, unhurried technique.

## A Simple Request, an Uneven Result

The client's ask was clear and straightforward: short, square, white nails. By her account, the shaping was not done with enough care, and the set came out lumpy and uneven rather than the crisp, clean look she wanted. Short square is one of the most classic shapes, and getting it right is all about patience, filing each nail to the same length and straight edges, and applying the product in thin, smooth layers so the surface stays even. A lumpy finish usually points to product applied too thick or too quickly, both of which a slower, more deliberate hand prevents. A quick check across all ten nails before finishing would have caught the unevenness while it was still easy to fix.

## Right Before a Big Day

What made the disappointment sharper was the timing. This was just two days before her graduation, a day she would remember and photograph, so the nails were part of a milestone rather than an ordinary touch-up. When a client mentions a big occasion like a graduation, that is exactly the cue to slow down and give the set extra care and a final once-over, because there is little room to redo it in time. Treating those moments as special is one of the simplest ways to turn a client into a loyal one.

## Editorial Perspective

The encouraging part is that this is entirely about technique and attention, both of which are easy to strengthen. First, take the time to shape carefully: match every nail in length and keep square edges clean and straight, so a simple request comes out looking polished. Second, apply in thin, even layers to keep the surface smooth, and do a final check across all ten nails before the client leaves. Third, give special occasions the extra care they deserve, since a client coming in two days before graduation is trusting the salon with a milestone. Handled with a patient, even hand, this classic set would have been exactly what she pictured, and the perfect finishing touch for her big day.
