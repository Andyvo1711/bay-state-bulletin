The Massachusetts Nurses Association scheduled an open-ended strike for November 12 at Brigham and Women's Hospital, which the union described as the largest nurses' strike in state history, Boston.com and CBS Boston reported.

The union represents more than 4,000 nurses at the hospital. They authorized an open-ended strike September 24 with 93% support, after a one-day strike in July that Mass General Brigham followed with a four-day lockout. Hospital officials said they planned to bring in replacement nurses to keep the hospital open during a strike.

Nurses' priorities included wage increases, affordable health insurance, limits on temporary and travel nurses, and patient care protections. A strike would have required 10 days' notice under federal labor law.
