Boston-based Wayfair reported third-quarter 2026 results on November 4, with net revenue up about 9.7% to $3.4 billion and U.S. revenue up 11.4% to $3.0 billion. Active customers totaled 22.2 million, up 4.7%.

The company posted net income of $62 million, compared with a net loss of $99 million a year earlier. Adjusted diluted EPS was $0.93 versus $0.70, ahead of analyst expectations of roughly $0.84 to $0.87, and adjusted EBITDA was $236 million, a 6.9% margin that the company called its best third quarter since 2020. International revenue fell 2.3% to $380 million. Wayfair expects fourth-quarter revenue to grow at a high-single-digit rate.
