Boston Scientific announced October 7 that Joseph M. Fitzgerald will become executive vice president and chief operating officer on January 1, 2027. Fitzgerald, with the company since 1990, will oversee global operations, business units and regions.

Also effective January 1, the company will report three segments: Interventional Therapies, Rhythm Solutions and MedSurg, led by Lance Bates, Nick Spadea-Anello and Stephen Morse, respectively, all reporting to Fitzgerald. The Penumbra business will be integrated into Interventional Therapies.

The board also approved finance leadership changes on October 1, effective March 1, 2027. Emily Woodworth moves from chief accounting officer to senior vice president, global finance controller, and finance transformation, and Mark Bickel becomes senior vice president, global accounting controller, and chief accounting officer. The company said the move does not reflect any disagreement over financial reporting or controls.
