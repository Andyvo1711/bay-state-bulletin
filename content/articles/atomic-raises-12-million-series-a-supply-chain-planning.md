Atomic, a Boston company building an AI-native supply chain planning platform, raised a $12.5 million Series A, according to a September 29 Raise Track briefing. Klass Capital and Madrona led the round.

The company's platform targets supply chain planning with artificial intelligence. Further details on the round were not available at publication.
