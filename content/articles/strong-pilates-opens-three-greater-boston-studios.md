STRONG Pilates, an international brand founded in October 2019, is opening three studios in Greater Boston, with Back Bay and Fenway slated for November 14, 2026. A Kendall Square opening date has not been announced.

The studios feature proprietary Rowformer and Bikeformer machines and 45-minute, low-impact classes for all levels. The brand has more than 60,500 members and 130 studios in 15 countries as of September, and expects over 150 by January 2027.
