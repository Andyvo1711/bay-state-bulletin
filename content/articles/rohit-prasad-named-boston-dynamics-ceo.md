Boston Dynamics named Rohit Prasad as chief executive effective October 7, the company said. Prasad was previously senior vice president and head scientist for Alexa and artificial general intelligence at Amazon, where he spent 12 years and helped develop the Nova generative models. Before Amazon, he spent 14 years at Raytheon BBN Technologies.

He is the company's third CEO and is expected to join its board once approval is complete. Robert Playter stepped down in February after six years as CEO and more than 30 years with the company; CFO Amanda McMaster served as interim CEO. Prasad will lead the company's Physical AI strategy.

"Boston Dynamics is uniquely positioned to advance Physical AI through its world-class expertise," Prasad said. Hyundai became sole owner after buying the remaining shares from SoftBank in July, and Hyundai plans to deploy 25,000 Atlas humanoid robots across Kia factories and other plants, beginning in 2028. The company, founded in 1992 as a spin-off from the MIT Leg Lab, opened a robotics center at Hyundai's electric vehicle plant near Savannah, Georgia, in September.
