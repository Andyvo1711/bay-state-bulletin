Boston-area gyms and studios are growing again, the Boston Globe reported August 9. Massachusetts had over 150,000 more gym members last year than in 2021, and 1.9 million fitness-facility members in 2025, matching 2019, per the Health and Fitness Association.

Healthworks Group's president said membership across its brands is up 20% since 2019, a record. SoWa Health and Wellness, which opened in the South End in 2023 with 400 members, now has more than 2,000, each paying $190 a month. About 35% of members at six of Beacon Hill Athletics Clubs' seven locations are Gen Z. Planet Fitness reported $1.3 billion in 2025 revenue.

"It's almost like the new club is working out," said Geoffrey Janey, founder of JET Boston-Brooklyn, noting younger members mix workouts with nights out.
