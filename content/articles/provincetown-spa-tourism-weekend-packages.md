Several spas in Provincetown have introduced off-season weekend getaway packages this year aimed at attracting visitors from Boston seeking a quieter Cape Cod retreat outside the peak summer crowds.

The packages typically bundle overnight accommodations at partner inns with a full spa treatment menu, positioning Provincetown's quieter fall and winter atmosphere as a selling point for visitors seeking a more relaxed experience than the town's busy summer season.

"We're never going to compete with July and August for volume, and we've stopped trying to," said the owner of a Provincetown spa that partnered with a nearby inn on a new off-season package. "What we can offer this time of year is genuine quiet and a slower pace, and it turns out there's real demand for that from people who visited in summer and wanted to come back when it wasn't so busy."

The packages have found a particular audience among repeat visitors from the Boston metro area looking for a shorter drive than more distant wellness retreat destinations, while still offering a meaningful change of scenery from urban and suburban daily life. Spa owners said early bookings have skewed toward couples and small groups of friends rather than solo travelers.

Regional tourism officials have expressed interest in supporting the wellness tourism packages as part of broader efforts to extend Cape Cod's tourism season beyond its traditional reliance on the summer beach months. Spa owners said they plan to expand package offerings into additional shoulder-season months if demand continues to build.
