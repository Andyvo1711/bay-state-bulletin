The MGH Institute of Health Professions became Mass General Brigham University of Health Professions on September 1, according to the school.

The school cited its growth over the past decade, including graduate education and undergraduate pathways, as the reason for the "university" designation, and said its mission and values are unchanged. Signage, website and systems changes begin in summer 2026 and roll out in phases, with the full transition aligned to its 50th anniversary in 2027.

Degrees awarded before September 1, 2026 remain MGH Institute degrees, and transcripts will carry a note about the name change.
