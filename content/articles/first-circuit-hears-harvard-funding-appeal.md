The U.S. Court of Appeals for the First Circuit heard oral argument October 5 in the Trump administration's appeal of a ruling that restored about $2.7 billion in federal research funding to Harvard University.

According to the Boston Globe, the administration challenged the jurisdiction of the district court's ruling and argued the government should decide who gets taxpayer funding. Harvard's outside counsel, Paul Clement, reiterated that the cuts violated the First Amendment. Harvard Magazine reported the government largely set aside the Title VI civil rights questions that made up most of its earlier case and focused instead on jurisdiction.

U.S. District Judge Allison Burroughs ruled for Harvard on September 3, 2025; final judgment followed October 20, and the government appealed December 18. Harvard Magazine reported Harvard received most of the funding it was owed for work performed during the appeal window. The First Circuit was set to hear Harvard's separate international-student case the next day. No ruling or settlement had been reported as of publication.
