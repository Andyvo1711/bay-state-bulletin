Boston Public Schools has proposed closing three schools in June 2028, citing declining enrollment, with the School Committee scheduled to vote December 2.

The proposal names Charlestown High School, where enrollment has fallen about a third in three years, from 820 students to 559; Greater Egleston High School in Roxbury, an alternative school with 76 students; and the East Boston Early Education Center, which serves pre-K through first grade. Officials cited a 25% drop in elementary-age students in East Boston over the past decade. Pre-K classrooms there would stay in the current building and be incorporated into the McKay school, creating a pre-K through eighth-grade pathway.

Charlestown High students would begin transitioning to another high school during the 2028-29 school year. Greater Egleston students would be helped to graduate within 18 months, with those needing more time transferring to another alternative school. The broader plan also relocates Boston Green Academy to the former South Boston High School building in fall 2028 after renovation, and the Boston Teachers Union has noted that Boston International Newcomers Academy and Boston Adult Technical Academy would move to the former Cleveland Middle School building.

At its November 4 meeting, the School Committee asked for an equity analysis using the district's racial equity planning tool and raised concerns about the effect on alternative education schools and students with disabilities. A separate round of closures was approved in December 2025 for 2027.
