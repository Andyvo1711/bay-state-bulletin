Mass General Brigham Health Plan said it will remove Dana-Farber Cancer Institute from its Medicare Advantage network on October 1, Healthcare Dive reported September 8.

The insurer cited "the challenging environment affecting Medicare Advantage plans nationwide." Commercial plans are not affected, and Dana-Farber remains in the Mass General Brigham Health Plan commercial provider network.

The move follows the planned end of the long-standing partnership between Mass General Brigham and Dana-Farber. In 2023, Dana-Farber announced it would end the partnership in 2028 and build a cancer hospital with Beth Israel Lahey Health; Mass General Brigham said it would spend $400 million renovating its own cancer care facilities. Contract disputes between hospitals and Medicare Advantage insurers are becoming more common as providers seek higher reimbursement and insurers resist paying more.
