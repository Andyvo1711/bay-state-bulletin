The Massachusetts Department of Revenue reported preliminary October collections of $3.046 billion, $119 million or 4.1% more than October 2025, according to figures as of November 4.

Income tax collections were $1.749 billion, $187 million or 12.0% above benchmark but $23 million or 1.3% below October 2025. Withholding was $1.466 billion, $103 million or 7.6% above benchmark but $60 million or 4.0% below a year earlier. Sales and use tax totaled $907 million, $94 million or 11.5% above benchmark.

Year to date since July 1, collections were running about $521 million, or 3.7%, above benchmark, according to the Worcester Business Journal. Growth over last year came mainly from non-withholding income tax, sales tax and corporate and business taxes. November figures are due December 3.
