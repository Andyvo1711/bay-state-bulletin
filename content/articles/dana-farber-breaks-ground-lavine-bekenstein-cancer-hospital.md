Dana-Farber Cancer Institute broke ground on the Lavine Bekenstein Cancer Hospital on Monday, October 19, in Boston's Longwood Medical Area.

The hospital will have 300 beds, including 30 intensive care unit beds and a 16-bed observation suite, across 450,000 square feet and 10 inpatient floors. It is being built on the former Joslin Diabetes Center site on Brookline Avenue and is scheduled to open in 2031. The naming gift came from Josh and Anita Bekenstein and the Jonathan and Jeannie Lavine family. One trade publication put the project cost at $1.68 billion; other sources did not confirm the figure.

Dana-Farber is transitioning its inpatient cancer-care partnership to Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center, where hospitalized patients will be housed until the new hospital opens. Dana-Farber said it will hire about 2,400 workers for the hospital over the next four years.
