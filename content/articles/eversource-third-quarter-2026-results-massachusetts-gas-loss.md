Eversource Energy reported third-quarter 2026 earnings of $0.92 per share, or $346.0 million, on November 4, compared with $0.99 a year earlier and a consensus of $0.97. Revenue was $2.98 billion.

The natural gas distribution segment lost $43.4 million, versus a $16.8 million loss a year earlier, which the company traced to lower revenues at its Massachusetts gas businesses after a change in seasonal rate design shifted when quarterly revenue is recovered. Electric distribution earned $200.9 million. Higher income tax rates and interest expense, and disallowed storm costs from a July 29 Connecticut regulatory decision, also weighed on results.

The company now expects 2026 non-GAAP earnings of $4.62 to $4.70 per share, versus a prior range of $4.57 to $4.72, and maintained its $26.5 billion capital plan.
