Massachusetts Department of Revenue Commissioner Geoffrey Snyder announced October 5 that preliminary September tax collections totaled $5.457 billion, $858 million or 18.6% more than September 2025 and $147 million above benchmark.

Income tax collections were $3.234 billion, $50 million or 1.6% above benchmark. Withholding totaled $1.525 billion, $32 million or 2.1% above benchmark, and corporate and business tax collections were $1.141 billion, $74 million or 7.0% above benchmark.
