The Massachusetts Nurses Association said nurses and clinicians would rally outside the Westin Seaport on September 22 and 23 as Mass General Brigham hosted its AI-focused World Medical Innovation Forum.

The rallies came as Brigham and Women's nurses prepared for an open-ended strike vote, which they approved September 24. Gov. Maura Healey had been scheduled to speak at the forum September 22 but declined the invitation, according to reports.

Mass General Brigham has said it must strengthen its operating margin and has begun layoffs and program changes. The union has argued the system should invest in safe patient care.
