GE Vernova, headquartered in Cambridge, reported third-quarter 2026 results on October 28, raised its 2026 financial guidance and agreed to acquire Trench Group, a global leader in high-voltage grid equipment, for $9.0 billion.

The company now expects 2026 revenue of $46.5 billion to $47.5 billion and ended the quarter with cash of approximately $17 billion. Additional details of the quarter were not available in the coverage reviewed for this article.
