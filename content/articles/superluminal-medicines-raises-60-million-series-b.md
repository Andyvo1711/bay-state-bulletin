Boston drug-discovery company Superluminal Medicines announced a $60 million Series B on September 3. BVF Partners led the oversubscribed round, joined by investors including RA Capital Management, Nvidia and Eli Lilly, with new investors Deep Track Capital and Perceptive Advisors.

The funds will advance its lead program, a selective, biased MC4R agonist for rare genetic forms of obesity and hypothalamic obesity, into a Phase 1 trial expected by the end of 2026. Superluminal uses AI, protein dynamics and structural biology to target G protein-coupled receptors and has a partnership with Eli Lilly in cardiometabolic disease and obesity.

The financing brings its total raised to $213 million across three rounds. The program's selectivity claims remain preclinical and have not been tested in humans.
