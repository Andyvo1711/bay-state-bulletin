Massachusetts' unemployment rate held at 4.4% in July, the state said August 21, with payroll jobs down 4,600 following a revised gain of 6,000 in June. Labor force participation stayed at 65.3%, and the national rate was 4.1%.

The Boston Globe reported total employment rose just 5,800, or less than 0.2%, since July 2025, and that excluding gains in health care and private education the state lost 6,800 jobs. The July job loss was later revised, with a September 18 report putting it at 4,000.
