Students, teachers and parents at Charlestown High School have pushed back on Boston Public Schools' proposal to close the school in June 2028, WGBH and Boston 25 News reported.

District data show enrollment at the school fell 32% over three years, from 820 students to 559. Many students told School Committee members they want to stay with their friends and graduate from Charlestown High despite a longer commute if it closes. A change.org petition argues the school serves many students with special education needs, and City Councilor Coletta Zapata has called for an oversight hearing because the school is beloved in the community.

Mayor Michelle Wu's administration says the closures are tied to low birthrates and falling immigration. The School Committee is scheduled to vote December 2 on the proposal, which also includes Greater Egleston High School and the East Boston Early Education Center.
