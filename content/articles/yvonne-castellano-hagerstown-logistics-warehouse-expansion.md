Yvonne Castellano, chief executive of Hagerstown-based Castellano Distribution Partners, opened the company's third regional warehouse this month, a 350,000-square-foot facility that positions the firm to capitalize on Western Maryland's growing status as a mid-Atlantic distribution hub.

Castellano took over the family logistics business from her father fifteen years ago, when it operated a single warehouse handling regional grocery distribution, and has since expanded the company into e-commerce fulfillment as retail distribution patterns shifted. Hagerstown's position near major interstate corridors has made it an increasingly attractive location for distribution companies serving the broader Mid-Atlantic and Northeast markets.

"My father built this business around grocery trucks going to regional stores, and I've had to rebuild significant parts of it around pallets going straight to somebody's front door," Castellano said. "Hagerstown's location is honestly the biggest reason we've been able to make that shift without relocating."

The new warehouse will add approximately 180 jobs, primarily in fulfillment and logistics coordination roles, and includes automated sorting equipment that Castellano said was necessary to remain competitive with larger national logistics firms that have also been expanding into the region. Washington County officials have actively courted logistics investment in recent years as the area's industrial land availability has drawn interest from companies priced out of more expensive markets closer to Baltimore and Washington.

Castellano Distribution Partners now employs approximately 640 people across its three regional facilities. Castellano said the company is evaluating a fourth location but has not committed to a timeline or site.
