Massachusetts' annual sales tax holiday ran Saturday, August 8, and Sunday, August 9, after the Legislature set the dates June 11. A 2018 law made the holiday permanent but requires lawmakers to vote on its dates each year.

Most retail items up to $2,500 purchased in Massachusetts for personal use on those two days were exempt from sales tax, including online purchases. Meals, motor vehicles and motorboats did not qualify, and items had to be purchased and paid for during the weekend.
