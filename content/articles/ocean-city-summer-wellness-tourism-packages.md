Several Ocean City hotels have introduced bundled wellness packages this summer combining spa treatments, sunrise beach yoga classes, and healthy dining options, an effort to capture a larger share of tourism spending from visitors increasingly interested in wellness-oriented vacation experiences rather than traditional beach trip itineraries.

Hotel operators said the packages respond to booking data showing a growing subset of summer visitors searching specifically for wellness amenities when choosing accommodations, a trend that has accelerated coastal hotels' investment in spa facilities and fitness programming that previously played a secondary role to beach access and pool amenities.

"Guests used to just want a room close to the beach and maybe a decent pool, and now a meaningful number of our bookings are coming from people specifically asking what spa services and fitness classes we offer before they even ask about room views," said the general manager of an Ocean City oceanfront hotel that added a wellness package option this year. "That's a real shift in how people are thinking about a beach vacation."

The packages typically bundle a spa treatment, a fitness or yoga class, and a healthy dining credit at the hotel's restaurant into a single pricing tier, an approach hotel operators say increases average revenue per guest compared with visitors who might otherwise only book a standard room. Several hotels have added beachfront yoga instructors specifically to support the new packages during peak summer months.

Ocean City's tourism board said the wellness tourism trend aligns with broader hospitality industry patterns showing growing visitor interest in health-oriented travel experiences. Hotel operators said they plan to expand wellness package offerings further if summer demand continues justifying the additional staffing and programming investment.
