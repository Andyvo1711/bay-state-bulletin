Lizbeth Heyer became chief executive of Massachusetts-based 2Life Communities on October 2, succeeding Amy Schectman, who retired after about 16 years leading the senior-housing nonprofit.

The board unanimously approved Heyer as Schectman's successor after the retirement was announced in January. Heyer joined 2Life in 2014 and has led the development, acquisition and renovation of more than 1,000 units.

Schectman had led the organization as an advocate for affordable and middle-market senior living across Massachusetts. Sources differ on whether her tenure was 15 or 16 years.
