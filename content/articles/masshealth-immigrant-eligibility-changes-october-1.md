New federal Medicaid rules took effect October 1 that end MassHealth eligibility for some lawfully present immigrants in Massachusetts.

Under the rules, adults generally keep eligibility only if they are lawful permanent residents, Cuban/Haitian entrants or COFA migrants. Estimates of the number affected vary: MassHealth materials cite about 2,500 members in one set of figures and roughly 7,300 people in another. Pregnant people keep MassHealth Standard through pregnancy and 12 months postpartum. Some who lose MassHealth may qualify for ConnectorCare Plan Type 2 through December 2026 if their income is at least 100% of the federal poverty level. MassHealth began mailing notices in mid-September.

Gov. Maura Healey launched a statewide outreach campaign August 11 to help eligible residents navigate new Medicaid requirements. More changes are coming in January 2027, when work requirements and six-month eligibility checks begin and ConnectorCare rules change again.
