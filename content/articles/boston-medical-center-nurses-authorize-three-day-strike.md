Nearly 2,000 nurses at Boston Medical Center voted to authorize a three-day strike, with close to 90% support, their union announced September 11.

Pay is a central issue. According to the union, BMC nurses start at $38.05 an hour, compared with $41.71 at Brigham and Women's and $43.01 at Tufts Medical Center. The vote comes as BMC deals with financial losses and upcoming federal Medicaid cuts. Federal labor law requires 10 days' notice before a hospital strike, and the sides planned to return to the bargaining table September 25.

In a separate vote the week of September 18, about 160 more BMC nurses represented by the Massachusetts Nurses Association also voted overwhelmingly to authorize a three-day strike. The votes came as roughly 4,000 Brigham and Women's nurses authorized an open-ended strike.
