G2O Spa + Salon, a Back Bay day spa at 33 Exeter Street, closed permanently after business Sunday, August 30, according to the spa's announcement and local guides.

Joymark, Inc., which does business as G2O, filed a WARN notice covering 55 Boston workers, with a notice date of July 6 and a layoff date of August 31, according to layoff trackers. The spa had operated for more than three decades.
