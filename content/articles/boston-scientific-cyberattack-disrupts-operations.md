Boston Scientific told the Securities and Exchange Commission on August 26 that a cybersecurity incident caused a global disruption to its operations, including its ability to process and ship customer orders.

The company, which said it activated incident-response protocols and brought in third-party experts, had not determined whether the incident was reasonably likely to be material. Most reports say it was detected August 25. A later update said manufacturing and shipping remained disrupted, and the company addressed customers and patients in a September 1 statement. The company has not named a threat actor or said whether data was taken. Its stock fell roughly 3.5% to 6% in premarket trading.

In its October 28 third-quarter release, CEO Mike Mahoney credited global teams for restoring operations.
