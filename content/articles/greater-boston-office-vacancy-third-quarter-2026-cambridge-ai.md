Greater Boston office vacancy reached about 24% in the third quarter, with the region losing roughly 1.26 million square feet of occupied space during the first nine months of the year, according to Newmark figures cited in local reporting. Cambridge gained occupied office space this summer while Boston lost ground.

Cushman & Wakefield put Cambridge's overall office vacancy at 20.5% and cited AI firms among its third-quarter transactions; the Boston Globe, in an October 19 report on whether AI and robotics could fill Cambridge's empty offices, put Cambridge at about a quarter vacant, compared with 23.2% in Boston's central business district. The figures differ by methodology and geography and are not directly comparable.

In life sciences, CBRE said the Greater Boston lab market slipped back in the third quarter after posting positive net absorption in the second.
