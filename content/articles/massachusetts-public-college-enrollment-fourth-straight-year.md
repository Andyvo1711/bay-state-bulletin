Preliminary fall 2026 data presented to the Massachusetts Board of Higher Education on October 20 show undergraduate enrollment at public colleges and universities up 2.2% to nearly 176,000 students, the fourth consecutive year of growth, Gov. Maura Healey's office announced.

The gain includes the first enrollment increase at Massachusetts' nine state universities since 2013. Enrollment at the 15 community colleges rose another 3.9% to nearly 90,000 students. New transfer enrollment grew 16.6% at state universities and 3.8% at UMass campuses compared with last fall, while UMass system enrollment was level, according to WBUR.

Healey and Senate President Karen Spilka highlighted the state's free community college program as a driver of the increase. The Department of Higher Education cautioned that fall 2026 figures are preliminary estimates collected before the final data submission and exclude students who enroll later in the fall.
