The ownership group of the Boston Celtics, led by Bill Chisholm, announced a $200 million gift to Boston Children's Hospital on October 29, the largest in the hospital's history, WBUR and the Boston Globe reported.

According to WBUR, the money will support an innovation fund for "daring" research ideas, advanced training for nurses, and family programs that promote healthy lifestyles. The Globe reported that about half the total is tied to Rob and Karen Hale, whose previously announced $100 million went toward a new psychiatric campus in Brighton.

On October 1, Bank of America announced a $10 million grant to Boston Children's to help establish that behavioral health campus at the Franciscan Children's site. The Globe reported the money will go toward the $640 million hospital, and coverage said the project will create between 150 and 200 permanent jobs.
