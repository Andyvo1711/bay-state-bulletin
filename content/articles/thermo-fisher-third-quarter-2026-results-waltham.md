Thermo Fisher Scientific, based in Waltham, reported third-quarter 2026 revenue of $12.14 billion on October 21, up 9% from $11.12 billion a year earlier, with organic growth of 6%.

GAAP diluted earnings per share were $4.64, up 9%, and adjusted EPS was $6.68, up 15% and above the $6.40 analyst consensus. GAAP operating margin was 18.6% versus 17.4%, and adjusted operating margin was 24.5% versus 23.3%. Laboratory Products and Biopharma Services revenue rose to $6.863 billion from $5.970 billion, while Specialty Diagnostics fell to $1.098 billion from $1.174 billion.

The company completed the divestiture of its microbiology business during the quarter. CEO Marc Casper called the quarter excellent.
