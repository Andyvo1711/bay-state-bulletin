Devon Park, the founder of Boston-based Harborline Restaurant Group, is opening the company's first Providence location this fall, expanding a brand built over the past decade around seafood-focused dining near Boston's North End waterfront.

Park opened his first restaurant, a small seafood spot near the Boston waterfront, twelve years ago after working as a chef in New York for most of his career. The concept, built around locally sourced New England seafood and a rotating menu tied to seasonal catch availability, expanded to three additional Boston-area locations over the following decade.

"I always said I'd only leave Boston if the right building came along in another New England city, and Providence finally gave us that," Park said. "This isn't about franchising a formula somewhere else, it's about bringing the same fishing fleet relationships we've built to a new dining room."

The new Providence location, situated in a renovated warehouse space, will seat roughly 180 guests and employ approximately 85 staff once fully operational. Park said the restaurant group has maintained direct purchasing relationships with several New Bedford and Gloucester seafood harvesters since its founding, a supply chain he described as central to the brand's identity.

Harborline Restaurant Group currently employs around 240 people across its Boston-area locations, and Park said the company generated approximately $30 million in revenue last year. He said further expansion beyond Providence is possible but not currently planned, citing a preference for growing deliberately rather than rapidly.
