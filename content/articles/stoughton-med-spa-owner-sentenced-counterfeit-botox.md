Rebecca Fadanelli, 40, of Stoughton, was sentenced September 2 to 46 months in federal prison and ordered to pay more than $1 million in restitution and forfeiture, the U.S. Attorney's Office for Massachusetts announced.

Prosecutors said she ran Skin Beaute Med Spa in Randolph and South Easton and performed more than 2,700 injection procedures on over 900 clients between 2021 and 2024 using counterfeit versions of Botox and Juvéderm imported from China and Brazil. WWLP reported she falsely claimed to be a licensed nurse. She pleaded guilty in April to four counts of importing merchandise contrary to law and counts of selling or dispensing counterfeit drugs and devices.

Authorities said she kept operating despite repeated warnings. Patients who believe they received such products between 2021 and 2025 can contact the FDA's Office of Criminal Investigations victim assistance program.
