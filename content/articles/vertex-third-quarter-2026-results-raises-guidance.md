Vertex Pharmaceuticals reported third-quarter 2026 revenue of $3.44 billion on November 2, up 12%, and raised its full-year revenue guidance to $13.15 billion to $13.25 billion from $13.1 billion to $13.2 billion.

U.S. revenue grew 10% to $2.17 billion and revenue outside the U.S. grew 16% to $1.27 billion, driven by continued cystic fibrosis demand and diversification into other disease areas. GAAP net income fell to $654 million from $1.1 billion a year earlier, mainly because of Crinetics acquisition expenses; non-GAAP net income held at $1.2 billion. Non-GAAP EPS was $4.76, slightly below the $4.80 of the year-ago quarter. Casgevy brought in $99 million and Journavx $58 million.

Cash and marketable securities were $9.8 billion after the Crinetics acquisition closed September 1. Vertex is preparing for a November 30 FDA decision date for povetacicept in IgA nephropathy.
