Boston-based State Street reported third-quarter 2026 results October 14, with total revenue of $4.004 billion, up 12.9% from $3.545 billion a year earlier, and diluted earnings per share of $3.69, up 32.7% from $2.78.

Assets under custody and/or administration reached a record $59.2 trillion, and assets under management a record $6.3 trillion, as of September 30. EPS was in line with the $3.68 consensus estimate. Fourth-quarter and full-year results are scheduled for January 15, 2027.
