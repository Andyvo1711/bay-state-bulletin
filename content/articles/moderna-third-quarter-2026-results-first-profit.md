Moderna reported third-quarter 2026 results on November 6, with revenue up 29% to $1.3 billion, including $780 million in the U.S. and $533 million internationally. Net income was $162 million, or $0.38 per diluted share, compared with a net loss of $200 million a year earlier.

Research and development expenses fell 27% to $583 million. Cash and investments were $8.0 billion as of September 30, up from $6.9 billion in June, reflecting $2.6 billion in net proceeds from convertible notes and related transactions. The Cambridge company raised its 2026 revenue growth expectation to 15% to 25% from up to 10%, and improved its GAAP operating expense outlook by about $0.2 billion.

Moderna cited positive Phase 3 data for intismeran and FDA approval of mFlusiva, its fifth approved product worldwide.
