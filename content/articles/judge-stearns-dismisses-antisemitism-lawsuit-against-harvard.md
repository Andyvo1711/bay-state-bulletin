A federal judge in Boston dismissed the Trump administration's antisemitism lawsuit against Harvard University on Thursday, August 13.

The Justice Department had sued under Title VI of the Civil Rights Act to recover billions of dollars in federal research grants awarded to Harvard since October 2023. U.S. District Judge Richard G. Stearns wrote that the cited incidents were "too isolated and episodic" to support a plausible inference of ongoing noncompliance. Most of the incidents came from the 2023-24 school year, with a few from March 2025, and the complaint lacked facts showing violations after June 30, 2025, he said.

Assistant Attorney General Harmeet Dhillon said the department disagrees with the ruling and is "assessing next steps." Harvard said it has taken substantive steps to address the root causes of antisemitism. The ruling is separate from an earlier decision ordering the administration to reverse more than $2.6 billion in cuts to Harvard, which the government has appealed.
