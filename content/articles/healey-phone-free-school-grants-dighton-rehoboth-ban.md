Gov. Maura Healey announced Monday that Massachusetts schools can apply for $1 million in cellphone-free school grants, funding that can go toward policy development, staff training and equipment such as phone pouches. Applications are due Oct. 21.

The money is part of a $4.5 million state Student Support and Well-being Systems initiative. A 2023-24 pilot provided nearly $1.3 million to 77 districts. "Cell phones and social media have fundamentally changed the environment our kids are growing up in," Healey said.

The Dighton-Rehoboth Regional School District announced a ban covering pre-K through 12th grade, requiring phones to be powered off or silenced and out of sight during the school day. Pioneer Valley Regional, Methuen and Fall River also restrict phones. In August 2025 the state Senate approved a statewide bell-to-bell ban for the 2026-27 school year; in April the House passed a broader bill that would also bar children under 14 from social media, and that bill was sent back to the Senate, where it remains.
