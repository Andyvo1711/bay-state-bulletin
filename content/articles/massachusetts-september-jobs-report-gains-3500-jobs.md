Massachusetts' unemployment rate held at 4.3% in September, matching the revised August rate, the state reported October 16. The national rate rose 0.1 point to 4.2%.

Payroll employment grew by 3,500 in September and was up 16,300 jobs from a year earlier. Labor force participation rose to 65.4%. Local data released October 20 showed rates decreasing in nine of the state's twelve labor market areas. The Boston Globe noted hiring has picked up over the past year even though employment has barely grown since Gov. Maura Healey took office.
