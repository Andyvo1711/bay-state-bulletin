Boston Public Schools opened the 2026-27 school year on Tuesday, September 8, welcoming more than 54,000 students back to classrooms, according to GBH News.

Boston Globe coverage highlighted Holland Elementary, which rejoins the district for the first time in more than a decade. Parents and students described excitement about the new year mixed with some concern, GBH reported.

Later in the month, the Boston School Committee took up a strategic roadmap and public comment on proposed changes for students with limited or interrupted formal education, according to a summary of its September 16 meeting.
