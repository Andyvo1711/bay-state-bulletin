Boston-based Solstice Oncology reported a $225 million Series A on September 9. RA Capital Management led the round, with Canaan Partners, Forbion and other investors participating.

The immuno-oncology company aims to treat cancer earlier, in the neoadjuvant setting. Its lead candidate, porustobart, is a second-generation, Fc-enhanced CTLA-4 antibody now in a Phase 2 trial with pembrolizumab for stage II-III microsatellite-stable colon cancer. The company is pursuing a second indication it has not disclosed. Caroline Loew, PhD, is CEO.
