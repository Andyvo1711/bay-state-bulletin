Nearly 2,000 nurses at Boston Medical Center voted to ratify a new three-year contract, averting a planned strike, their union announced October 9, according to the Boston Globe.

The agreement covers nurses at the main campus and the affiliated Brockton Behavioral Health Center. Pay for first-year nurses rises from $38.05 to $42.63 an hour over the first two years, which the Globe said brings BMC in line with nearby hospitals; new-graduate nurses start at $41.71 an hour at Brigham and Women's and $43.01 at Tufts Medical Center. The union said the pay increases total $3.1 million more than management's previous wage offer.

In September, the nurses voted overwhelmingly to authorize a three-day strike over pay and staffing after bargaining for more than six months. About 160 BMC nurses represented by the Massachusetts Nurses Association also authorized a strike separately.
