Life Time has leased about 45,000 square feet at Commonwealth Pier in Boston's Seaport, alongside Fidelity Investments' new headquarters, the Boston Globe reported. Pembroke, Fidelity's real estate affiliate, is the landlord.

The club will be mainly on the third floor, with a street entrance off Seaport Boulevard and waterfront views. It will have no pool. Opening is expected in 2027, with about 150 employees and memberships expected around $369 a month. It will also be open to the public.

"Any building that's built on stilts over the ocean will give you a challenge or two," said Parham Javaheri, Life Time's head of real estate. Life Time has seven clubs in and around Boston and is considering four or five more.
