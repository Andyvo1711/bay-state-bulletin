Boston Scientific reported third-quarter 2026 net sales of $5.138 billion, up 1.5% on reported, operational and organic bases from $5.065 billion a year earlier. MedSurg sales were $1.733 billion and Cardiovascular $3.406 billion.

GAAP diluted earnings per share were $0.40, down from $0.51, and adjusted EPS was $0.73 versus $0.75. For the full year, the company guided to roughly 4.25% reported net sales growth and adjusted EPS of $3.16 to $3.18, and projected fourth-quarter sales down 1% to 3%.

Chairman and CEO Mike Mahoney called it a challenging year and credited global teams for responding to the cybersecurity incident first reported August 26 and restoring operations. The release did not quantify the incident's financial impact.
